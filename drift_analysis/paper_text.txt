\paragraph{Drift analysis.}
Figure~\ref{fig:drift} tracks normalised BN parameter drift
$\|\theta_t - \theta_0\|_2 / \|\theta_0\|_2$ over the 75-block
truly continual sequence.
TENT accumulates large, monotonically increasing drift
(final drift: $0.169$), consistent with unconditional
entropy minimisation steadily moving parameters away from
the source calibration regardless of whether genuine shift
has occurred.
\ours{} exhibits a staircase pattern: drift increases sharply
at corruption boundaries (when JS$>\tau$ triggers adaptation)
and remains flat within stable corruption periods
(when the gate suppresses updates), reaching a final drift
of $0.065$ — $2.6\times$ smaller than TENT.
This directly visualises the mechanism by which JS gating
prevents drift accumulation: adaptation is selective rather
than continuous.
